Teaching & engagement

Teaching & Outreach

I have taught programming and machine learning, supervised research projects, facilitated AI learning programs, and contributed to academic peer review and public discussions.

Teaching and training

I have taught and tutored programming and machine learning through classes, laboratory sessions and introductory workshops. My instruction combines core concepts with implementation and practical exercises.

  • C programming: co-supervised laboratory sessions focused on writing, testing and debugging programs.
  • Machine learning: taught classes and tutorials on learning algorithms, model development and evaluation.
  • Introduction to AI with Python: delivered practical sessions using Python to introduce AI concepts.

Machine learning classes · UM6P International Student Community

Delivered in-person classes on Python programming and the foundations of machine learning.

Research supervision and mentoring

I have supervised research projects in machine learning for wireless localization, guiding work from the definition of research questions and selection of methods to implementation, experimental design and analysis of results.

Program facilitation

Emerging Leaders in AI · Black in AI

As a program facilitator, I have helped run sessions, engaged with participants and provided mentoring within the Emerging Leaders in AI program.

AI education and public engagement

AI training webinar series

Delivered a series of introductory webinars on AI fundamentals, practical applications and interactive exercises.

I have also participated in panels on AI in the workplace and on the relationship between research and development in Africa, with particular attention to connectivity and AI. Details are available under Panels & Forums.

Academic service

I served on the Technical Program Committee for IEEE WCNC 2024 and have reviewed papers for IEEE ICC and IEEE GLOBECOM, as well as manuscripts for IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, IEEE Internet of Things Journal and IEEE Sensors Journal.
