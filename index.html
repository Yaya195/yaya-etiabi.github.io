Yaya Etiabi PhD

Applied Machine Learning Researcher

Wireless Communications | Localization | Internet of Things (IoT) | Edge Intelligence

My research focuses on machine learning for wireless systems, at the intersection of Edge Intelligence and integrated sensing, communication, and computation (ISCC). I investigate how learning and inference can be made efficient at the network edge under resource constraints.

My background includes collaborative and privacy-preserving machine learning, wireless localization, and radio sensing, providing a foundation for my research on intelligent and resource-efficient wireless systems.

My current research explores the integration of intelligence, sensing, communication, and computation in wireless systems, building on earlier work in collaborative learning, wireless sensing, and localization.
