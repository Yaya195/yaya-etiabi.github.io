Background & interests

About me

I am a researcher working at the intersection of machine learning and wireless communications. I hold a PhD in Computer Science and an engineering degree in Electrical Engineering, with a specialization in embedded systems and telecommunications.

Academic and research background

My research began with distributed localization methods for Internet of Things (IoT) networks. During my PhD at Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), completed in 2025, I focused on machine learning for collaborative and privacy-preserving wireless localization. This work included federated learning, meta-learning and federated distillation methods for adapting localization models to different environments and reducing the communication required for collaborative training. I also pursued related research during an internship at ISEP in Paris.

My postdoctoral research at Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) in 2025–2026 broadened this work toward AI-native wireless systems and Edge Intelligence. I am particularly interested in the joint design of sensing, communication and computation for learning and inference, as well as radio-based sensing and localization. These directions build on my earlier research while addressing different questions at the intersection of AI and wireless systems.

Research approach

I work across the research process, from defining a problem and developing a method to implementing experiments and assessing the results. My work draws on wireless-system simulations and experimental datasets, with evaluations grounded in quantitative comparisons, including accuracy, adaptation, communication overhead and computational cost where relevant. The Research, Projects and Publications pages provide the technical details and research outputs.

Teaching and academic contribution

Teaching, mentoring and student research supervision are part of my academic experience. I have contributed to programming and machine learning education and guided student research from the development of research questions through implementation, evaluation and presentation of results.

I also contribute to academic peer review and conference technical program activities. More about this work is available on the Teaching & Outreach page.

Scientific communication

I have presented research at international conferences and academic events, including scientific communication competitions. I was a finalist in the IEEE ComSoc Three Minute Thesis competition at IEEE ICC 2023 and received presentation awards at UM6P Doctoral Days and a MOMI event at Inria. The Talks & Presentations page documents these activities.

Community engagement & personal interests

I am a founding member and Vice President of the International Network of Burkinabè Researchers (INBR). I also serve as Director of Scientific and Thematic Content at L'AURA.

I also enjoy distance running and have participated in two half-marathons.
